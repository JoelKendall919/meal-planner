# Training Plan

**Intake:** 1750 kcal/day

*Cardio-led: 1 strength session, 4 cardio sessions, 2 recovery days.*

---

## 1. Ground Rules

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Daily intake | 1750 kcal |
| Expected rate | 0.7–0.9 kg/week |

**Non-negotiables**
- 3 L water/day (more on long training days)
- 7–9 h sleep — the biggest lever on both hunger and recovery
- Weigh yourself every morning; judge the **7-day average**, never a single day
- 8,000–10,000 steps/day on top of training
- **170 g protein/day** — see the Meal Plan. Without it the training below cannot protect your muscle.

> **Note on the cardio-led approach.** One strength session a week sits at the low end for muscle retention in a deficit, so expect to lose *some* lean mass alongside the fat. Three things offset most of it: hitting your protein target, taking that single strength session genuinely close to failure, and keeping most cardio in Zone 2 rather than hammering every session.

---

## 2. The Week

Four cardio sessions, one full-body strength session. The strength day sits on Monday when you're freshest, and is followed by an easy day so it can actually be recovered from.

| Day | Session | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mon** | Full Body Dumbbells (the one strength day) | 55–60 min |
| **Tue** | Easy Run — Zone 2 | 35–40 min |
| **Wed** | Cycling — Intervals | 45 min |
| **Thu** | Rest / walk + mobility | 20–30 min |
| **Fri** | Run — Tempo or Intervals | 35 min |
| **Sat** | Long Ride *or* Long Run | 60–90 min |
| **Sun** | Full rest / gentle walk | — |

---

### Monday — Full Body Dumbbells

This is your only strength stimulus all week, so it has to cover everything and it has to be hard. **Take every working set to within 1–2 reps of failure.** That proximity to failure is what preserves muscle when frequency is low.

Warm up 8 min: 3 min easy bike or brisk walk, then arm circles, bodyweight squats, hip hinges, band pull-aparts.

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Targets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goblet Squat | 4 × 10–12 | 90 s | Quads, glutes |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 4 × 10–12 | 90 s | Hamstrings, glutes, lower back |
| Dumbbell Floor Press *(or bench)* | 4 × 10–12 | 90 s | Chest, triceps |
| Single-Arm Dumbbell Row | 4 × 10–12 each side | 75 s | Lats, upper back |
| Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 3 × 10–12 | 75 s | Shoulders |
| Dumbbell Reverse Lunge | 3 × 10 each leg | 75 s | Legs, single-leg stability |
| Lateral Raise | 3 × 15 | 45 s | Side delts |
| Hammer Curl | 3 × 12 | 45 s | Biceps, forearms |
| Plank | 3 × 45 s | 45 s | Core |

*Short on time? The first four exercises are non-negotiable. Drop from the bottom up.*

---

### Tuesday — Easy Run (Zone 2)

- 5 min brisk walk warm-up
- 30–35 min easy running — **you should be able to hold a conversation throughout**. If you can't, slow down or use run/walk intervals (4 min run / 1 min walk).
- 5 min walk cool-down, then calf and hamstring stretches

*Run/walk intervals are the smart start. Build the habit before you build the pace — joints first.*

---

### Wednesday — Cycling Intervals

- 10 min easy spin warm-up
- **6 × 2 min hard** (heavy resistance, hard breathing, can only manage a few words) with **2 min easy spin** between
- 8 min easy spin cool-down

Works on a spin bike, turbo trainer, or outdoors on a hill loop. Add one interval every fortnight, up to 10.

---

### Thursday — Active Recovery

20–30 min walk plus 10 min stretching: hip flexors, hamstrings, chest, upper back. This day exists so Friday and Saturday are quality sessions rather than survival.

---

### Friday — Run: Tempo or Intervals

Alternate weekly:

- **Week A — Tempo:** 10 min easy · **15 min at a "comfortably hard" pace** (you can say a short sentence, not a paragraph) · 10 min easy.
- **Week B — Intervals:** 10 min easy · **8 × 1 min hard / 90 s walk or jog** · 10 min easy.

---

### Saturday — Long Effort

Alternate weekly so you're not pounding the joints every week:

- **Week A — Long ride:** 75–90 min moderate, mostly flat with a couple of climbs. Easiest way to burn a big chunk of calories with low impact.
- **Week B — Long run:** 60 min steady and conversational. Run/walk is completely fine.

---

### Sunday — Rest

Full rest or a gentle 30 min walk. Prep food for the week — see the **Meal Plan**, §5 Sunday Prep.

---

### Progression

Every 2 weeks, do **one** of these:

- **Strength day:** add 1–2 reps per set, or add 2–2.5 kg per dumbbell once you hit the top of the rep range with clean form.
- **Cardio:** add 5 min to the long session, or add one interval to Wednesday/Friday.

Never add weight if form breaks down. Deload (halve the volume) every 6–8 weeks.

**Guardrail:** track the weight you use on goblet squats, floor press and rows. If those numbers slide for three weeks running, you're losing muscle rather than just fat — add a second short strength day (squat, press, row, RDL, 3 sets each) on Thursday and the slide will stop.
