**What changed in this version**

One 30 g whey shake a day, **added on top** of the food rather than replacing any of it. To keep the deficit intact, roughly 120 kcal of starch and oil came out of the meals each day — smaller rice and potato portions, the stir-fry noodles dropped entirely, less oil in the pan. Nothing that was carrying protein was touched.

|  | v6 (food only) | v7 (+ daily shake) |

| Protein | 165 g | **186 g** |
| Protein per kg bodyweight | 1.5 g | **1.7 g** |
| Calories | 1749 | 1753 |

At 186 g you're at the top end of what the evidence supports for holding muscle in a deficit, and well clear of the level where more stops helping. Carbohydrate is now 122 g a day, which is the real cost — if the long Saturday ride starts feeling flat, put the rice back up to 40 g on Friday and Saturday and skip the shake those two days.

## 2. The Week

## 3. Recipes

All figures calculated from raw ingredient weights. Vegetable quantities are given as a weight followed by the rough equivalent in whole items — the approximation is close enough, so there is no need to weigh them.

**Continue to weigh these, as errors here are costly:** oils, dry rice, couscous and lentils, nuts and nut butter, cheese, meat and fish. An unmeasured pour of oil is roughly three times a measured teaspoon, which is 90 kcal.

A 400 g tin of beans or chickpeas drains to about 240 g. All weights are raw unless stated.

### Weekend brunches

**W1 — Shakshuka (V)** · 755 kcal · 59 g protein · *25 min · Saturday*
> 5 eggs · 100 g passata · 80 g pepper *(1 small)* · 50 g onion *(½ medium)* · 50 g drained chickpeas *(¼ tin)* · 60 g feta · 50 g spinach *(2 big handfuls)* · 2 ml olive oil · 1 tsp each ground cumin and smoked paprika · 1 birds eye chilli · 2 cloves garlic · **100 g 0% Greek yoghurt to serve**

1. Soften the onion, pepper and garlic in the oil, 5 minutes.
2. Add the cumin, paprika and chilli. Cook 30 seconds.
3. Add the passata and chickpeas. Simmer 8 minutes until thickened. Do not shorten this step — it sets the texture of the dish.
4. Stir the spinach through until wilted.
5. Make five wells, crack an egg into each, cover and cook 6–8 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
6. Crumble the feta over. Serve the yoghurt alongside.

**W2 — Smoked Salmon Scramble + Yoghurt Bowl** · 676 kcal · 74 g protein · *20 min · Sunday*
> 4 eggs · 100 g smoked salmon *(use the whole pack)* · 60 g new potatoes *(4–5 small)* · 40 g spinach *(2 big handfuls)* · 75 g cherry tomatoes *(7–8)* · 3 ml olive oil · **Bowl:** 200 g 0% Greek yoghurt · 100 g frozen berries *(2 small handfuls)*

1. Halve the potatoes and boil 8 minutes. Drain.
2. Crush them into the oiled pan and fry until the edges are crisp, 5 minutes.
3. Push to one side. Add the tomatoes and spinach to wilt.
4. Beat the eggs with black pepper. Scramble over a low heat in a separate pan, removing from the heat while still loose — they continue to cook.
5. Fold the smoked salmon through at the end, off the heat.
6. Serve the yoghurt and berries in a bowl alongside.

### Weekday first meals

**L1 — Roast Chicken Salad** · 577 kcal · 59 g protein · *assembly only · Monday*
> 215 g roast chicken thigh meat, skin removed (from Sunday's D7) · 200 g leftover roast veg · 60 g new potatoes *(4–5 small)* · 50 g salad leaves *(3 loose handfuls)* · 75 g cherry tomatoes *(7–8)* · 4 ml olive oil · lime juice

1. Shred the cold chicken, discarding the skin.
2. Combine with the roast veg, potatoes, leaves and tomatoes.
3. Dress with the oil and lime juice.

**L2 — Leftover Turkey Chilli** · 674 kcal · 66 g protein — see D1. Reheat thoroughly. *Tuesday.*

**L3 — Leftover Tofu & Edamame Stir-Fry (V)** · 670 kcal · 61 g protein — see D2. Reheat in a hot pan rather than a microwave. *Wednesday.*

**L4 — Chickpea & Feta Couscous Bowl (V)** · 674 kcal · 54 g protein · *10 min · Friday*
> 115 g drained chickpeas *(½ tin)* · 15 g couscous (dry) · 50 g feta · 3 boiled eggs · 75 g cucumber *(6 cm length)* · 75 g cherry tomatoes *(7–8)* · 30 g red onion *(¼ medium)* · 1 ml olive oil *(or 8 g tahini)* · lime juice · **150 g 0% Greek yoghurt**

1. Pour 25 ml boiling water over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes, then fork through.
2. Boil the eggs 7 minutes, cool and peel.
3. Chop the cucumber, tomatoes and red onion. Combine with the chickpeas and couscous.
4. Thin the yoghurt with lime juice and the oil or tahini to make a dressing. Pour over.
5. Halve the eggs and place on top. Crumble the feta over.

The yoghurt provides 15 g of the protein and is a component of the dish rather than a garnish.

**L5 — Tuna Jacket Potato** · 634 kcal · 61 g protein · *15 min · Thursday*
> 300 g baking potato *(1 large)* · 200 g drained tuna in spring water *(2 tins)* · 25 g light mayo · 15 g spring onion *(2 onions)* · 50 g salad leaves *(3 loose handfuls)* · 5 ml olive oil

1. Microwave the potato 10–12 minutes, turning once. For a crisp skin, finish 10 minutes in a hot oven.
2. Mix the tuna, mayo and spring onion.
3. Split the potato, fill, and serve with the leaves dressed in the oil.

*Substitution:* jerk mackerel fillets in place of the tuna — omit the mayo to keep the calories level.

### Dinners

**D1 — Turkey Chilli + Rice** · 674 kcal · 66 g protein · *35 min · makes 2 portions*

Per portion: 250 g raw turkey mince · 40 g onion *(½ small)* · 40 g pepper *(½ small)* · 75 g passata + splash of water · 120 g drained kidney beans *(½ tin)* · 3 g ground cumin, smoked paprika and birds eye chilli · 2 cloves garlic · ½ stock cube · 4 ml olive oil · 22 g basmati rice (dry)

> **Batch:** 1 × 500 g pack turkey mince · 1 onion · 1 pepper · 150 g passata · 1 × 400 g tin kidney beans *(drains to ≈240 g — use all of it)* · spices · 1 stock cube · 8 ml olive oil

1. Brown the mince in the oil over a high heat. Remove and set aside.
2. Soften the onion and pepper, 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices, 30 seconds.
3. Return the mince. Add the passata, beans, stock cube and a splash of water.
4. Simmer 20 minutes.
5. Cook the rice separately.

Two portions exactly: Monday's dinner and Tuesday's first meal.

**D2 — Tofu & Edamame Stir-Fry (V)** · 670 kcal · 61 g protein · *25 min · makes 2 portions*

Per portion: 225 g firm tofu *(½ block)* · 170 g frozen edamame · 75 g pepper *(¾ medium)* · 60 g carrot *(1 small)* · 100 g tenderstem broccoli *(½ pack)* · 15 g spring onion *(2 onions)* · 1 ml sesame oil · 2 ml sunflower oil

**Sauce:** 15 ml soy sauce · 3 g peanut butter · 5 ml rice wine vinegar · dash fish sauce · garlic and ginger, whisked with a tablespoon of water.

> **Batch:** 1 × 450 g block firm tofu · 340 g frozen edamame · 1 pepper · 1 large carrot · 200 g tenderstem · double the sauce

1. Press the tofu 15 minutes under a weighted plate. This step determines the final texture.
2. Cube and toss in a teaspoon of cornflour.
3. Fry in the sunflower oil over a high heat until golden on all sides. Remove from the pan.
4. Stir-fry the carrot and broccoli 3 minutes, then the pepper and edamame 2 minutes.
5. Return the tofu, add the sauce and the sesame oil, toss for 1 minute.
6. Finish with the spring onion and sesame seeds.

The edamame provides 11 g of protein per 100 g. There are no noodles in this dish; the tofu and edamame give it enough substance without them.

**D3 — Salmon, New Potatoes & Greens** · 632 kcal · 56 g protein · *25 min · Wednesday*
> 200 g salmon fillet · 100 g new potatoes *(7–8 small)* · 150 g tenderstem broccoli *(¾ pack)* · 100 g green beans *(2 loose handfuls)* · 2 ml olive oil · **70 g 0% Greek yoghurt** whisked with lime and black pepper

1. Boil the potatoes 15 minutes. Add the greens for the final 4 minutes.
2. Place the salmon skin-side down in a dry non-stick pan over a medium-high heat, 4 minutes. Flip and cook 2 minutes. No oil is needed.
3. Whisk the yoghurt with lime juice and black pepper.
4. Dress the vegetables in the oil. Serve with the sauce.

The yoghurt sauce adds 7 g of protein.

**D4 — Steak, Sweet Potato Wedges & Broccoli** · 631 kcal · 59 g protein · *30 min · Friday*
> 225 g sirloin, fat trimmed · 200 g sweet potato *(1 medium)* · 200 g broccoli *(1 medium head, florets only)* · 6 ml olive oil

1. Cut the sweet potato into wedges, toss in most of the oil, roast at 200°C for 25 minutes.
2. Steam or boil the broccoli 5 minutes.
3. Season the steak. Fry over a high heat 2–3 minutes per side.
4. Rest the steak 5 minutes before slicing.

**D5 — Beef & Bean Burrito Bowl** · 635 kcal · 67 g protein · *25 min · Thursday · makes 2 portions*

Per portion: 250 g raw 5% beef mince · 120 g drained black beans *(½ tin)* · 40 g onion *(½ small)* · 12 g basmati rice (dry) · 50 g sweetcorn · 10 g grated cheddar · 30 g salsa · lime juice · pickled chillies

> **Batch:** 1 × 500 g pack beef mince · 1 × 400 g tin black beans *(drains to ≈240 g — use all of it)* · 1 onion · 1 small tin sweetcorn · cheddar · salsa

1. Cook the rice.
2. Brown the mince over a high heat. Add the onion and soften, 5 minutes.
3. Add the beans and sweetcorn, heat through 5 minutes.
4. Assemble over the rice. Top with salsa, cheddar, lime juice and pickled chillies.

Eat one portion, freeze the other.

**D6 — Lentil & Chickpea Dahl (V)** · 676 kcal · 65 g protein · *35 min · Saturday*
> 80 g red lentils (dry) · 75 g drained chickpeas *(⅓ tin)* · 50 g onion *(½ medium)* · 100 g passata + splash of water · 10 ml light coconut milk *(freeze the remainder in an ice-cube tray)* · 50 g spinach *(2 big handfuls)* · 2 ml sunflower oil · **350 g 0% Greek yoghurt on top**
>
> **Tempering:** ½ tsp cumin seeds · ½ tsp mustard seeds · 6 curry leaves · pinch asafoetida · 1 tsp ground coriander · 1 birds eye chilli · 2 cloves garlic · thumb of ginger

1. Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
2. Heat the oil. Add the mustard seeds and wait until they pop, then the cumin seeds, curry leaves and asafoetida for 30 seconds. Do not let them burn.
3. Add the onion, garlic and ginger. Soften 5 minutes.
4. Add the ground coriander and chilli, then the lentils, passata and 300 ml water.
5. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Add the chickpeas, coconut milk and spinach for the final 5 minutes.
7. Serve with the yoghurt on top.

No rice is served with this dish; the lentils provide the carbohydrate. The 350 g of yoghurt contributes 36 g of protein.

**D7 — Roast Chicken Thighs & Veg** · 576 kcal · 58 g protein · *50 min · Sunday · makes 2 portions*

> **Batch:** 4 large bone-in skin-on chicken thighs (≈800 g) · ≈500 g raw veg *(1 courgette, 1 pepper, 1 red onion)* — *roasts down to about 400 g* · 200 g new potatoes *(1 large handful, ~15)* · 6 ml olive oil

1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
2. Halve the potatoes and chop the vegetables. Toss in the oil and season.
3. Place the thighs skin-side up on top of the vegetables.
4. Roast 40–45 minutes until the juices run clear.

Roast with the skin on to keep the meat moist, but leave the skin on the plate — it is roughly 135 kcal across the two thighs. Eat two thighs on Sunday; the remaining two become Monday's L1.
